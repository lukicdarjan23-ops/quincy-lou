Questions

What people ask on the first call.

Why not hire someone in-house?

A good designer costs £45,000 a year before you’ve found a developer or anyone who reads analytics. Most firms need that trio for about eight weeks.

How long does it take?

Four to six weeks for most sites. The date is on the quote. The only thing that moves it is waiting on content.

What if we don’t like the design?

Revisions are unlimited inside the design stage. If two concepts in we’re still nowhere, you can stop and pay only for work done.

Do you write the words?

Yes. We interview you and two or three of your customers, then draft every page. You have the final say.

Can we edit it ourselves?

That’s the point. Prices, staff, opening hours, case studies — all yours to change, with a training session and a short video.

Who will we actually deal with?

Rob, from first call to launch. Not an account manager who passes messages along.
