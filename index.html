Let’s be honest

The four things you’re already thinking.

This is going to be expensive.

Sites start at £8,500 and most land between £8,500 and £22,000. You get the full number and the launch date in writing before you commit to anything, so the only surprise is the invoice being exactly what we said.

If one extra job a month covers it, it’s a cost. If it doesn’t, we’ll tell you on the review call and save you the money.

We haven’t got six weeks.

Most builds run four to six weeks and the date is fixed at quote stage, not hoped for. In twelve years we’ve missed it twice, both times waiting on content from the client’s side.

Genuinely urgent? We keep one 10-day sprint slot open each month for a single landing page that has to be live before a campaign starts.

We already have a designer.

Good. Keep them. Plenty of our work starts as a build-only job on somebody else’s designs, or an optimise retainer sitting on top of a site we never touched.

We’ll happily work alongside an in-house designer or your existing agency. Nobody has to be fired for us to be useful.

We tried this before and nothing changed.

Usually because nobody agreed what “worked” meant. We set the number we’re moving before the project starts, and it’s an enquiry, a booking or a call, never a bounce rate.

Then we stay for 90 days after launch and keep changing things. That’s the part the last agency skipped.

FAQ

Questions we get every week.

Why wouldn’t we just hire someone in-house?+

A decent mid-weight designer costs £45,000 plus employer costs, and you still need someone who can build and someone who can read analytics. Most businesses need that trio for about eight weeks a year.

Hire in-house when the work is constant. Use us when it isn’t.

How long does a website actually take?+

Four to six weeks for most sites, from kick-off to live. Bigger builds with a lot of content run eight to ten. You get the date in the quote and we hold to it.

The one thing that moves it is content. If your copy and photos are ready, we’re fast.

What if we don’t like the design?+

You get unlimited revisions inside the design sprint, so we keep going until it’s right rather than counting rounds at you.

If we’re still nowhere after the first two concepts, you can walk away and pay only for the work delivered. It’s happened three times in twelve years.

Do you write the copy?+

Yes, and we’d rather. The words do most of the selling, so writing them is part of the design job, not an add-on.

We interview you and two or three of your customers, then draft everything. You edit, you approve, you always have the final word.

Can our team edit the site after launch?+

That’s the point. Every site ships on a CMS with your content types set up properly, plus a training session and a short video for whoever joins next year.

Prices, staff, case studies, opening hours: all yours to change without ringing anyone.

What happens after the 90 days?+

Your call. Take the keys and run it yourself, or move onto an optimise retainer from £1,200 a month and we keep testing.

Either way you own the design files, the code, the domain and the hosting. Nothing is held hostage.

Do you work outside the UK?+

Regularly. Roughly a fifth of our work is in Ireland, the Netherlands and the US east coast.

We keep at least three hours of overlap with your working day, so anything further west than Denver isn’t a good fit and we’ll say so.
