A small studio for businesses
that run on enquiries.

We started Quincy Lou because the businesses we knew best, the trades, clinics and practices, kept being sold websites built to win awards instead of to make the phone ring.

Most of our clients have never hired an agency before, or they have and it went badly. Both are fine. We work in plain English, we put the price in writing before anything starts, and we measure success in enquiries, bookings and calls.

We stay small on purpose. Four people means the person who reviews your site is the person who designs it, and the person who builds it is the one who picks up when something breaks.
