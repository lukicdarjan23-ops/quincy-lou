Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026. Plain English, as short as we could make it.

Who we are

Quincy Lou Ltd is a website studio registered in England and Wales. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website. You can contact us about privacy at the email address at the bottom of this page.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we collect the details you choose to give us: usually your name, email address, information about your business and your website address. If you sign up for our newsletter we collect your email address. Our hosting provider records standard technical logs such as IP addresses and browser types.

Why we collect it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, to prepare a website review if you ask for one, and to send the newsletter if you subscribed. Our legal basis is your consent or our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with services we use to run the business, such as our website host, email provider and form handling, and only as far as needed to provide those services.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted after 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as the law requires for accounting purposes. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in each email.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in future, this page will be updated to explain what it collects.
