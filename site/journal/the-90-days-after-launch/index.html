Why the 90 days after launch matter more than launch day

A new website is a guess about what your customers want. The three months after it goes live are when you find out how good the guess was.

Launch day feels like the finish line. It is closer to the start. Until real people use the site, every decision is based on interviews, experience and educated assumptions.

What we watch

We watch three things: where people arrive, where they stop, and what they do just before they get in touch. Session recordings show the moments of hesitation that no analytics dashboard will.

Small changes, one at a time

Most improvements are not dramatic. A clearer button label, a price moved higher up the page, a photo of the actual team instead of a stock image. Made one at a time, each change can be measured.

That is why every project we build includes ninety days of tuning. It is not an upsell. It is the part of the job that turns a nice website into a useful one.
