What our free website review actually covers

A twelve-minute recording, three priorities and a rough idea of what each is worth. Here is exactly what to expect.

When you send us your address, one of us opens your site on a phone and on a laptop and records the screen while talking through it. No automated audit tool, no forty-page PDF.

What you get back

  • A recording of around twelve minutes
  • The three changes we would make first, in order
  • A rough idea of what each change could be worth to you

What happens next

Nothing, unless you want it to. Plenty of people take the recording, make the changes themselves and never hire us. That is a perfectly good outcome.
