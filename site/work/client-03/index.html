A law firm that put its fees on the website

The problem

Every enquiry opened with the same question about price, and good prospects were leaving before asking it.

What we did

Fixed fees went on every service page, each practice area got its own page, and the contact form went from eleven fields to four.

What changed

Completed enquiry forms rose 164% in ninety days, and the partners say first calls are shorter because the price conversation has already happened.
